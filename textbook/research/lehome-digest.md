## (a) The Competition

**Identity & organizers.** VERIFIED [PAPER, SITE]: The LeHome Challenge 2026 is an ICRA 2026 competition — "Challenge on Garment Manipulation Skill Learning in Household Scenarios," billed as the "1st Simulation-Driven Competition on Deformable Object Manipulation." Organized by Ruihai Wu, Yuran Wang, et al. (Peking University, Lightwheel, partners). Official code: github.com/lehome-official/lehome-challenge; simulator paper: "LeHome: A Simulation Environment for Deformable Object Manipulation in Household Scenarios," arXiv 2604.22363.

**Task & robot.** VERIFIED [PAPER §1.1]: Fold a single garment lying flat on a table with a bimanual SO-ARM101 setup (The Robot Studio / Hugging Face LeRobot ecosystem): two 6-DOF arms, 12-dimensional joint action space, 30 Hz in sim / 20 Hz in the real round; three RGB cameras (one overhead, one per wrist; overhead depth available but Larchenko didn't use it). Four garment types: long-sleeved tops, short-sleeved tops, long pants, shorts.

**Simulator.** VERIFIED [PAPER §1.1]: NVIDIA Isaac Lab (built on Isaac Sim), on organizer-released environment, assets, and success checker.

**Success criteria.** VERIFIED [PAPER §1.1, §6.1]: Binary, geometric, keypoint-based. Each garment carries keypoints; success = conjunction of pairwise keypoint-distance conditions: "proximity" pairs must come within a threshold, "spread" pairs must stay beyond one. 5 conditions for tops, 4 for pants (paper §6.1 says 4 for short pants and 4 for long pants, which he expanded to 7 for his own reward). No partial credit in the online round. The sim also provides a shaped "niceness" reward he did not use.

**Rounds, timeline, scale.** VERIFIED [PAPER §1.1, SITE]:
- **Online (simulation) round, Feb 1 – Apr 30 2026**: open, continuous submissions evaluated in Isaac Sim, public leaderboard ranked by overall success rate. **62 teams** submitted. Per garment type, 20 instances: 10 *seen* (organizers released a scripted-policy behavior-cloning dataset for them), 10 *unseen* (2/type public-but-no-data, 8/type fully private). 80 garments total, each evaluated 10 times; ranking averages equally across the four types.
- **Real-world final, June 1–5 2026, ICRA Vienna**: top 8 sim teams, on-site physical robot; four arenas evaluated in parallel (June 2). Different criteria: **partial credit** (e.g., one folded sleeve), jury-scored composite; both success rate and per-step quality scored; unseen garments carried a **50% score bonus**; max total 1080 points. 5 garments/type: 3 seen (in the organizer real BC dataset), 2 unseen.
- Prizes VERIFIED [SITE]: Phase I $2,000 + hardware for top 3; Phase II $10,000 / $5,000 / $3,000.

**Protocol detail.** VERIFIED [PAPER §1.1]: Garment category is *not* given at evaluation — the policy must infer it.

**Organizer-provided data.** VERIFIED [PAPER]: scripted-policy BC dataset for the 10 seen sim garments; env/assets/success checker; for the real round, a 500-episode (~187k frames) real-robot BC dataset collected on a rig slightly different from both his rig and the evaluation rig.

## (b) The Solution

### Base policy & lineage
VERIFIED [PAPER §1.3, §4]: Starts from the policy his team built to win the **BEHAVIOR-1K Challenge 2025** (arXiv 2512.06951), itself an extension of **π0.5** (Physical Intelligence, arXiv 2504.16054): frozen **SigLIP-So400m/14** encoder → **Gemma-2B** prefix transformer → **Gemma-300M** flow-matching action expert emitting 30-step, 12-dim joint-delta chunks (1 s at 30 Hz). Three cameras at 224×224. FAST action tokens in the prefix as a training-only representation shaper. Carried over from BEHAVIOR-1K (explicitly *not* claimed as contributions): no language input at all, correlated flow-matching noise (empirical action covariance, Cholesky, shrinkage β=0.5), soft inpainting between chunks, cross-layer KV-cache mixing, multi-sample flow matching (5 samples/batch item share one prefix pass). New for LeHome: auxiliary heads, garment-type input token, advantage conditioning, AdaRMS multi-signal conditioning, Exclusive Self-Attention (XSA — subtracts each token's own-value projection from attention output), smooth per-timestep action normalization (σ_d(t)=a_d+s_d·√(t+e_d) fit, since deltas grow ~√t). He stresses this is an engineering case study with essentially no ablations.

**Attention layout.** VERIFIED [PAPER §4.1]: hierarchical groups — images + *current query* token; state (12 joints, 256-bin discretized) + garment-type token; advantage token; FAST tokens + FAST query (training-only, stripped before the action expert). Key choice: the current query sits in the *image* group, so all prediction heads work from pixels alone (no proprioceptive shortcut; garment-type head can't copy the input token).

### Auxiliary heads ("the policy is its own value function")
VERIFIED [PAPER §5]: All current-frame heads are **linear probes off one learned query token (1×2048)**. Heads / targets / losses:
- **success** — sigmoid, P(episode succeeds), BCE on binary outcome, RL rollouts; label smoothing toward per-garment mean SR (α=0.05); last 20 frames of successful episodes get **20× BCE weight** (tail-bias fix).
- **checkpoint** — sigmoid, P(reach mid-episode checkpoint) (legacy, unused downstream).
- **garment type** — softmax(4), all data; >99% accurate late in training.
- **completion** — sigmoid, target t/T, MSE, **successful episodes only**; policy-stable.
- **time-to-completion** — 30-step TD bootstrap target (τ_t = τ̂_{t+30} − 30/600 on success); legacy, aligned with π*0.6, trained but unused.
- **keypoint distances** — raw linear, 21 outputs = per-garment-type slices of the success checker's ratios d⁽ⁱ⁾=dist_i/threshold_i (top_long [0:5], top_short [5:10], pant_long [10:17], pant_short [17:21]); non-matching slices NaN-masked. Explicitly framed as a **cheap world-model substitute** ("predict the few numbers that matter"); needs sim-privileged targets.

**Future-prediction heads** (t+30): a training-only FAST query, plus an **FM query** at the tail of the action-expert suffix that reads the denoising actions — predicting future keypoint distances and a **success residual** Δ_success = y − sg(P̂_success) (MSE, raw linear; stop-grad protects the V-like head) — an action-conditional **Q-like** head. Future-loss weighted by (1−t), samples with flow-time t>0.5 excluded. Loss weights (final sim config): action FM 1.0; success 0.05; ttc 0.05; completion 0.02; garment 0.02; checkpoint 0.001; keypoint 0.02; FAST 0.01; wm_fast 0.01(×3); wm_flow success 0.1 / completion 0.05 / keypoint 0.02. Extra weight decay 0.001 on head kernels. No stop-gradient into the backbone.

### AWR + RECAP
VERIFIED [PAPER §2.2–2.3, §4.3]: PPO/GRPO's log-prob policy gradients "don't transfer cleanly to flow-matching VLAs," and push-away updates shove predictions off the narrow action manifold; conditioning/reweighting methods only redistribute mass toward actions the policy already produces (trade-off: little exploration — acceptable here). Advantage is consumed two ways:
1. **AWR through the sampler, not the loss**: P(sample frame i) ∝ e^clip(A_i, −2, 2); the FM loss itself is plain unweighted MSE. Equivalent in expectation, more data-efficient (low-weight frames are never decoded/loaded). Aux heads are debiased with importance weights w_i = 1/(N·p_i·T_ep(i)). BC/DAgger sources use failure-rate-proportional priority P ∝ e^{3(1−SR_garment)}.
2. **RECAP-style advantage conditioning** (from π*0.6, arXiv 2511.14759): a single learned **advantage token** (not bins) in its own attention group, plus an advantage vector added into **AdaRMS** conditioning (c = MLP(posemb(t)) + g[garment_type] + 1[adv active]·a; zero-init). Gating during training: A<0 → token always masked (neutral); A≥0 → masked stochastically, P(neutral) ramping 0.5 at A=0 → 0.1 at A≥2. This unlocks **classifier-free guidance** at inference.

Note vs. ground-truth notes: conditioning is implemented as a *gated learned token + AdaRMS vector*, not discrete advantage bins/tokens.

### Asynchronous flywheel via Hugging Face Hub
VERIFIED [PAPER §2.1, §3.1]: three independent components communicating **only through HF Hub**: (1) training worker (one GPU; loop = download new rollouts → recompute advantages over all rollout datasets → ~1000 train steps → upload checkpoint every ~500 steps); (2) any number of rollout workers (pull latest checkpoint; 3–5 parallel Isaac Sim instances each behind one shared stateless policy server; scaling = start another machine); (3) manual DAgger station. No sync barriers; background HF-sync daemons. Episodes saved with values predicted at collection time plus debug videos with reward/value/advantage overlays. Datasets never merged: runtime multi-dataset sampling with per-source shares; rollout datasets decay ×0.98/iteration (floor 0.1); BC share fixed. **Checkpoint rollbacks** (3 in round 1, 1 in round 2) to escape local optima — likened to π*0.6's systematic restart.

### Reward & advantage
VERIFIED [PAPER §6]:
- **Dense reward from the success checker**: intermediate checkpoints built from the task's own keypoint conditions (extra mid checkpoint for both tops and long pants; none for shorts). Mid checkpoint = 0.5 reward; full success → cumulative 1.0. For tops, the first 0.5 is granted *gradually*, proportional to reduction of the primary proximity distance (running-min formulation).
- **Failure withdrawal**: on failure, all accumulated reward withdrawn (Σr = 1[success]), spread uniformly over frames after the reward peak — episode return stays binary while checkpoints give within-episode temporal credit.
- **Value from the success head**: V(s_t) = P(success|s_t) − R_t^cum. **Damped baseline** V̂ = α_s(P − R^cum), **α_s = 0.5**, CUPED-inspired (control variates); v2 derives the estimable optimal θ* = ρ(V̂,G)·σ(G)/σ(V̂) and admits the fixed 0.5 was a mistake-that-worked (true per-garment coefficient ranged ~0.4–0.8, median ≈0.5). Damping means checkpoint rewards are only partially cancelled: δ_t ≈ (1−α_s γ)r_t + α_s(γS̄_{t+1} − S̄_t). EMA smoothing (α=0.2) on predictions; offline "value tail correction" interpolates last K=30 frames toward the known outcome.
- **Completion shaping**: potential-based, Φ_t = α_c·C̄_t, α_c = 0.5 — stable, works even when P(success) saturates >90%.
- **GAE** over both channels: γ=0.999, λ=0.99, computed offline before each iteration.
- **Staleness blend**: predictions recorded at collection time and never re-predicted; final advantage **Ã_t = w·A_t^s + (1−w)·A_t^seg + Φ_t^c**, w = min(sampling share, 1) — as data decays, GAE fades into a **GRPO-style outcome-only segment advantage** A^seg: episodes split at first checkpoint; each segment's return compared against per-garment empirical baselines (checkpoint rate p_cp, SR), scaled G(n)/n to match GAE magnitudes. Completion shaping stays at full strength (policy-stable). Normalized by a global std from unbiased rollouts, clipped to [−2,2].
- **Precision boost**: top 20% of successes per garment by *tightness* (worst per-condition margin at final frame) get +0.3 advantage on every frame.

### Rollout collection
VERIFIED [PAPER §3]: an episode takes ~30 s in Isaac Sim, so throughput engineering dominates. Strategies: **random**, **full**, **curriculum** (prefer garments near a configurable target SR; generalizes the ~50%-difficulty selection of arXiv 2506.05316), **success replay** (restore step-5 physics snapshot of a success, saved with prob 1−SR, re-run with heavier augmentation, keep only successful replays), **semi-success replay** (restore near-success state, open-loop replay to it, hand to live policy), **hard mining** (restore failure snapshots, taken when EMA success prediction drops >0.12 from a running max >0.25). Only random/full/curriculum count toward SR statistics. Early termination on success; stuck detector (state/action/value variance); watchdogs. Environment augmentation engine: episode-level (texture swap, LAB color remap, pose/scale/roughness, camera pose/focal jitter, base jitter, table texture, dome light — light regime p=0.4 in round 1; aggressive p=0.8 for replays and sim-to-real) + per-step (color tint, arm color, light). Sim DAgger existed but was of limited use (teleop-through-sim is hard; policy outgrew the operator).

### Inference-time knobs (all VERIFIED [PAPER §7])
Chunk H=30, S=10 Euler denoising steps; stateless server, all state client-side. **Seven** per-garment-type knobs tuned online with a **factorized Thompson-sampling bandit** (Beta posteriors per arm; reward = success − per-type mean SR; only full/random episodes update it; posteriors decay each iteration; frozen for submission): execution length n_e, playback stretch k, anchor length n_a, inpainting onset t_ip, CFG scale α, noise temperature τ, best-of-N count N. (The blog summary said "six"; the paper enumerates seven — trust the paper.) Final sim values (top_long/top_short/pant_long/pant_short): n_e 5/5/3/3; playback steps 5/5/3/3; n_a 6/3/3/3; t_ip 0.4/0.4/0.5/0.5; **guidance α 7/7/9/7** (started searching 0–2; the bandit kept pinning the top arm until ~7–9 — "surprisingly high"); τ 0.9/0.7/0.7/0.7; N 2/3/3/3. CFG runs conditional+unconditional action-expert passes over a shared prefix KV cache (v̂ = v_u + α(v_c − v_u)) — only doubles the cheap expert. Best-of-N scored by the FM-query Δ_success averaged over the two guidance passes, retry-with-larger-batch if all candidates negative; N>1 consistently helped, >3 didn't (mostly avoids rare bad chunks at multimodal bottlenecks — the head's outcome correlation was ≈0 yet it still helped). Soft inpainting active only while flow time > t_ip. Garment-type bootstrap: warm-up inference + majority vote over first chunks, then latched.

### Sim-to-real (final round)
VERIFIED [PAPER §9]: ~3 months for sim, ~1 week for sim-to-real. Zero-shot failed — the sim policy overfit even to image-resize pipelines (640→224 vs 640→320→224 was distinguishable by the aux heads and dropped SR). No access to the evaluation rig: effectively sim → his robot → their robot. Two levers: **align** (make domains similar) and **diversify** (randomize). Recipe: start from a *late-but-not-latest* checkpoint (latest = most sim-overfit); **strip the model** — keep training action objective + garment-type + completion heads; freeze success/checkpoint/TTC; remove keypoint and both world-model heads, advantage conditioning, CFG, best-of-N (no advantage on real; real inference = chunk + soft inpainting + garment bootstrap). **Training mix** (three buckets, fixed batch shares): organizer BC 500 eps/187k frames @60% (×1.00 sampling rate); his home teleop (321 eps) + DAgger (471 eps) 30% (×0.21); sim success-replays rendered into real-format data 1,723 eps/625k frames @10% (×0.05). Heavy augmentation: per-camera-independent color jitter/gain/gamma, blur, sensor noise, per-camera crop/rotate/zoom/translate, cutout, camera dropout, state noise/dropout. **Per-source speed factors** resample each source's time axis to match organizer-BC motion intensity: BC ×1.0, home teleop ×1.5, sim ×0.65, DAgger ×2.0 (per-chunk depending on human vs autonomous anchor). **Camera-overlay alignment tool**: drives the robot to a dataset frame's joint state and overlays live cameras on the dataset frame (also sim-render-on-real); he shared it with organizers/teams and it was used to align the official evaluation environments. Deliberate rig randomization (moving cameras, recalibrating, lighting changes) for robustness. **HIL DAgger was the main tactic**: two leader + two follower arms (46 cm apart per repo), three-pedal foot switch, leaders track followers for jump-free takeover; per-frame sampling weights as a quasi-advantage — human-correction frames highest, distant autonomous frames low, the 5-s window before a takeover ramps to zero. A LeRobot 0.4.x units bug (normalized ±100 vs degrees state encoding, ~10% skew) was found two days before the deadline and fixed by post-processing. Kids'-size garments (he only realized on assembling the real rig).

### Results
VERIFIED [PAPER §8, §9.12]: **Online round: 1st of 62 at 79.63%** overall (per-type 74.5 / 70.0 / 80.5 / 93.5), +6.1 pp over 2nd (Shubham @ Vorwerk 73.50%; 3rd Dum-E 73.38%; 4th SCUT-Unlimited 73.13%). **Real final: 2nd, 865/1080** points (1st sZs 895; 3rd Dum-E 762.5). Final sim data pool: ~12,500 rollout episodes / ~4.3M frames over ~140 sessions (cumulative episodes a few times larger). Checkpoints: huggingface.co/IliaLarchenko/lehome_sim and /lehome_real; code github.com/IliaLarchenko/lehome_solution (Apache 2.0). Discussion highlights: unseen generalization was barely an issue; exploration/recovery for flow-matching policies remains the open problem; his estimate that a single unified sim+real pipeline could reach 90%+.
